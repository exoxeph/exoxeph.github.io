<Section id="prior" title="Prior problem">
  Backdoored checkpoints are a supply-chain risk for openly shared models. Published work showed that merging
  a suspect model with other models can reduce backdoor behavior.
</Section>

<Section id="method" title="Method, in public terms">
  Weight interpolation between a suspect model and a clean model with a mixing weight. Selective versus global
  averaging as a concept. Backdoor behavior is measured as attack success rate and task behavior as clean
  accuracy.
</Section>
